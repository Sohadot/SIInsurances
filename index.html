01 / The thesis

The important shift is not that AI becomes more intelligent. It is that intelligence becomes operational. It begins to act.

A / AUTHORITY

Systems receive permission.

Agents are delegated scopes, credentials, budgets and operational authority.

→
B / ACTION

Systems create consequences.

They transact, route, execute, select, reject, commit and interact with other systems.

→
C / LIABILITY

Risk acquires a new locus.

Responsibility must be traced across humans, organizations, models, agents and infrastructure.

The old question was:
what was insured?

A world of autonomous systems introduces another question: who — or what — possessed the authority to cause the insured event? When agency fragments, insurance has to map the fragments.
